# Guide-First Redesign

## What We're Building

Reframe AI Software Factory Mastery from a course-like curriculum into a complete field guide for designing, building, operating, governing, and improving an AI software factory. The Markdown corpus remains authoritative, but the public experience will expose the whole system through a clear guide sequence, a visual atlas, a readable full-chapter view, and a lightweight reference index.

## Why This Approach

The repository already contains substantial technical depth. The failure is presentation: learning paths, progress state, modes, reading-time labels, and heavy filters obscure the information and make the product feel like a training course. A guide-first structure preserves the technical corpus while making it possible to understand the entire system, move between overview and detail, and retain the architecture through first-party diagrams.

## Key Decisions

- The primary journey is **Understand → Design → Build → Prove → Operate → Improve**.
- Every chapter opens in its complete form. No mode filters hide portions of the source.
- The chapter reader removes completion tracking, course progress, and reading-time emphasis.
- A new visual atlas recreates the useful concepts from supplied reference graphics as accessible, responsive, first-party diagrams.
- The visual atlas covers lifecycle, the twelve production-AI disciplines, runtime/orchestration, agent patterns, memory, protocols, governance, loop reliability, observability, and engineering-attention altitude.
- The topic index uses only direct search and one area selector; advanced filtering is removed.
- Existing architecture, evidence, maturity, labs, and Mission Control case studies remain available as deeper references.
- Public content continues to exclude the specifically disallowed product terms.

## Success Criteria

- A first-time reader can explain what the factory is, how it works, and where to start from the homepage.
- The complete guide is readable in one stable sequence without choosing a persona or mode.
- The supplied infographic concepts are represented in readable diagrams with direct links to canonical chapters.
- Technical depth remains intact; redesigning navigation does not delete the underlying information.
- Desktop and mobile readers can navigate, scan, and read without horizontal overflow or tiny text.

## Next Steps

Implement the guide hub, visual atlas, simplified reader and index, then run the complete content and browser verification suite.
