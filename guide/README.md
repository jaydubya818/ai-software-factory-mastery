The Executive Architect Playbook grows around these connected areas:

| Area | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vision and Purpose | Define who I am becoming and why | Active |
| Character and Leadership | Build judgment, courage, accountability, and trust | Planned |
| Executive Communication | Explain difficult ideas clearly to every audience | Planned |
| AI Software Factories | Master the operating model and its implications | Planned |
| AI Engineering | Master models, agents, tools, context, evaluation, and runtime systems | Planned |
| Systems Thinking | Understand boundaries, feedback, constraints, and failure | Planned |
| Business and Startups | Turn technical capability into durable value | Planned |
| Product Strategy | Choose valuable problems and ship coherent products | Planned |
| Organizational Design | Build effective human-agent organizations | Planned |
| Financial Freedom | Build ownership, resilience, and long-term optionality | Planned |
| Health and Longevity | Protect the capacity required for a long mission | Planned |
| Relationships | Remain present, trustworthy, and connected | Planned |
| Decision Journal | Improve judgment through retained decisions and outcomes | Planned |
| Books and Lessons | Convert reading and experience into usable principles | Planned |
| Interview Playbooks | Communicate experience without hiding behind jargon | Source material preserved |
| Personal Principles | Maintain the rules I will not trade away | Active |

## Writing rule

Every substantial topic should answer:

- What is the enduring principle?
- Why does it matter?
- What do I believe?
- What did I observe in practice?
- Which case study or source changed my thinking?
- What tradeoffs remain?
- Can I explain, implement, debug, and teach it?

## Mission Control as a case study

Mission Control is referenced, not copied. A study note should include the
document or code path, the commit studied, my interpretation, the tradeoff I
observed, and the lesson I want to retain.
